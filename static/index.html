    A MYSTERY FOR TWO. OR THREE.
    Follow the words.
    Find your common ground.
    1. Each player starts with a different word. You’re all looking for the same hidden mystery.
    2. On your turn, select any word in your own collection. Try a word that connects to it.
    3. A strong enough connection lands. Its position shows how close it is to the mystery, and the turn passes.
    4. A weak connection reveals nothing. Keep trying from that word, or choose another launch point.
    5. Compare everyone’s clues. Use the suspects board to gather hunches, then play them when you can make the connection.
    6. Land on the mystery, or a very close neighbour, to solve it together. Only successful words count.
    Left and right show proximity, with Word Golf’s curved distance scale. Vertical spacing separates words for readability. A backward move can be a useful new route.
